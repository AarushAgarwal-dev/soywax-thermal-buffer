[0:00.7 - 0:19.2] Can soy wax smooth out heat spikes in compact AI hardware? This simulation compares three builds of a small AI chip with a fan heatsink: the stock heatsink, the same heatsink with 20 grams of soy wax, and that wax cast into aluminum foam. Each build runs three short AI jobs, then a long one.

[0:48.1 - 0:53.8] With wax in aluminum foam, the chip stays at or below 76 degrees, and at full speed.

[1:18.4 - 1:20.5] Now, an 8 minute job at full load.
[1:21.3 - 1:27.9] The stock heatsink holds full speed for just 52 seconds. The simple wax pack holds it for 71 seconds.

It is passive, with nothing to power or control. These are simulation results, so the next step is a bench test.
